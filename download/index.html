Jacket?

Take a jacket or not.
One tap, one answer.

Jacket? checks the hourly “feels like” forecast for where you’re going and the hours you’ll be out, then gives you one big answer. No charts to read.

Free · No account · No ads · No tracking · Google Play version coming soon

Made to answer one question well

🧥

One big answer

TAKE A JACKET or NO JACKET NEEDED, with “Close call” when it’s borderline.

🕒

The hours you’ll be out

Right now, the next 3 hours, the rest of today, or this evening. It warns you when a mild afternoon turns into a chilly night.

🙋

Tuned to you

Tell it if you get cold easily or run warm, and what you’ll be doing. It learns from how your outings went.

🌧️

Rain is separate

A waterproof layer isn’t always a warm one, so rain and snow get their own advice.

🔍

Shows its working

“Why this result” lays out the hour-by-hour forecast behind every answer.

🔒

Private by design

Your settings stay on your phone. Locations are rounded to about 1 km before the forecast request.

How to install

Android: the APK

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Jacket?.

Any phone: the web app

  1. Open takeajacket.com.
  2. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app.
  3. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.

The APK and the Google Play version are signed with different keys. If you install this APK and later want the Play version, uninstall this one first.
