## Full description (max 4000)

Should you take a jacket? Open the app and get one big answer: TAKE A JACKET or NO JACKET NEEDED.

No charts to read, no numbers to interpret. Jacket? checks the hourly forecast for where you're going and the hours you'll be out, then compares the "feels like" temperature with your own jacket point.

MADE FOR YOU
• Tell it whether you get cold easily, run warm, or are about average
• Pick your usual activity: standing, walking or running
• Optionally add your age range, sex and background to fine-tune the answer
• Tell it how your outings went, and it slowly learns your personal jacket point

THE RIGHT TIME AND PLACE
• Right now, the next 3 hours, the rest of today, or this evening
• Use your location or search any city, before you travel
• Warns you when it turns cold later, like a mild afternoon and a chilly night
• Rain and snow get separate advice: a waterproof layer isn't always a warm one

HONEST ANSWERS
• "Close call" when it's borderline
• "Why this result" shows the hour-by-hour forecast behind every answer
• Storm, ice and extreme-cold notices that point you to official warnings
• Never guesses when forecast data is missing

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your settings stay on your phone
• Locations are rounded to about 1 km before the forecast request

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

## Data safety form (answers that match the app)

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
